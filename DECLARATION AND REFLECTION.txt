procedural 3D robotic arm homework. I start by working through
the problem myself. I write down what the program has to do,

the assignment until I already know what I am building and how

notes to Cursor, using the Grok model, and ask it to turn those
notes into a build plan. I then apply that plan myself: I write
the code as far as I can from my own notes. I use GenAI again
only to double-check what I wrote, not to replace that step.

GenAI is there to organize a plan from my notes and to check
